Model 01
Financial Markets
Equities, ETFs, and cryptocurrencies. Questions take the form "Will [ticker] close above $[target] by month end?"

Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM)

We model price as a continuous stochastic process. The probability that price S reaches target K by time T follows the Black-Scholes framework:

Core formula
P(ST > K) = Φ(d) d = [ ln(S₀/K) + (μ − ½σ²)·T ] / (σ·√T) where: Φ = standard normal CDF S₀ = current price (live from Yahoo Finance / Binance) K = target price (auto-calculated, see below) μ = daily log-return mean (252 trading days) σ = realised daily volatility (252 trading days) T = trading days remaining until month end

Step-by-step: how a market is built

1
Fetch live price history
252 daily closing prices fetched from Yahoo Finance (primary) → Stooq (fallback). For crypto: Binance → CoinGecko.
2
Calculate realised volatility
Daily log-returns rᵢ = ln(Sᵢ/Sᵢ₋₁) computed. Mean (μ) and standard deviation (σ) derived over the trailing 252-day window.
3
Set target price
Target starts at current price. GBM probability is calculated. If P > 70%, the target is raised in 0.5% increments until P ≤ 70% (R10 ceiling).
4
Generate question and YES%
"Will AAPL close above $[target] by month end?" — YES probability shown, NO = 100 − YES.
5
Daily recalculation at 4:01pm ET
After US market close, all financial probabilities are recalculated using the new closing price. The 4:01pm ET trigger ensures end-of-day data is available (R8).
⚠️
Rule R10 — 70% probability ceiling. No financial market will display a YES% above 70%. If the GBM model returns a higher probability, the target price is automatically raised until the probability falls to ≤ 70%. This ensures markets remain competitively tradeable and avoids trivially certain outcomes.

Model 02
Football — League Matches
Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, Scottish Premiership. Questions: "Will [Home] beat [Away]?"

Weighted Elo Model

We use a weighted Elo rating model with six input factors. Raw Elo win probability is adjusted by form, venue, and season context.

Base Elo probability
P(home wins) = 1 / (1 + 10^((Raway − Rhome) / 400)) Then weighted adjustment: P_adjusted = w₁·P_elo + w₂·P_form + w₃·P_goals + w₄·P_home + w₅·P_h2h + w₆·P_pos

Model 03
Knockout & Bracket Matches
Champions League, Europa League knockout rounds. Higher-weight model — single-game elimination changes the probability landscape.

Modified Elo for Knockouts

In a two-legged or single-leg knockout, form and home advantage have higher weighting. Recent Champions League/Europa form is given more weight than league position.

Knockout weight adjustment
Is_knockout = true → weights adjusted: Elo: 40% (↑ from 35%) Recent form: 25% (↑ from 20%) Goals scored: 15% (unchanged) Home: 10% (↓ — neutral venue likely) H2H: 10% (↑ — opponent-specific) Position: 0% (↓ — irrelevant in knockout)

Bracket auto-advance (R13)

When a knockout market resolves, the engine automatically creates the next-round market based on the winner. For example, when the UCL semi-finals resolve, the Final market is generated with updated probabilities using the confirmed teams' Elo ratings.

Model 04
Tennis
Grand Slam matches. Surface-adjusted Elo model accounting for clay, grass, and hard court performance.

Surface-Adjusted Elo

ATP/WTA overall Elo is adjusted by surface-specific win rates. A clay-court specialist will have their clay probability significantly boosted vs their raw Elo on a hard court (R20).

Surface adjustment
P_adjusted = w_elo · P_elo + w_surface · P_surface_winrate + w_ranking · P_ranking + w_h2h · P_h2h + w_form · P_recent_form + w_tournament · P_tournament_history

Surface win rates (R20)

Surface-specific win rates are stored in tournaments.json under surface_win_rates. These are hardcoded from ATP/WTA historical data and updated seasonally.

Model 05
FIFA World Cup 2026
72 group stage fixtures across 12 groups (A–L). Knockout bracket markets will be generated as teams advance.

FIFA Elo Ratings

International matches use FIFA Elo ratings stored in tournaments.json under fifa_team_elo. These ratings reflect performance across all competitive international fixtures including qualifiers, weighted by opposition strength.

World Cup probability
P(home wins group match) = 1 / (1 + 10^((Raway − Rhome) / 400)) + home/neutral venue adjustment (+0% for neutral site) Ceiling: 89% max, 11% min (R2 guard)

Market Rules
Operational rules governing market lifecycle, suspension, and resolution.

R5 Kick-off suspension

Sports markets suspend at kick-off time. No new trades accepted during live play. Markets resume in a result pending state after the final whistle.

R6 Result pending state

After a match ends, the market enters Result Pending (amber) while the official result is confirmed. Markets resolve and pay out once the score is verified.

R7 Month-end suspension

Financial markets suspend 24 hours before month end to prevent trading on near-certain outcomes. All positions are settled at the official closing price.

R8 4:01pm ET recalculation

After US equity markets close, all financial probabilities recalculate using the day's official closing prices. This triggers automatically each trading day.

R9 Auto-resolution

Markets resolve automatically when the engine detects a final result — either from openfootball (sports) or the daily closing price (financial). Payouts settle immediately.

R10 70% probability ceiling

No market displays YES% above 70%. The target price or line is automatically adjusted upward until the probability falls to ≤ 70%, ensuring all markets remain competitively tradeable.

R13 Bracket auto-advance

When a knockout market resolves, the next-round market is automatically generated using the confirmed winner's Elo ratings for updated probability calculations.

R23 Data provenance

Markets display a cyan border when probabilities are derived from config/fallback data rather than a live API fetch. This is fully transparent — the model is the same, only the input freshness differs.
